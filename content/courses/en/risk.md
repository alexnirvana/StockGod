# Understand risk and trading costs

Your goal is to consider fees and allocation, then record a plan and a reflection.

## Price differences are not net results

If an order buys CNY 10,000 of shares and later sells them for CNY 10,000, the net result is a loss after fees.

Always inspect the order result and cash ledger. This teaching model includes configurable commissions, transfer fees, and a sell-side tax. These are versioned teaching assumptions.

## Allocation changes the impact

With CNY 100,000 in total assets, investing CNY 10,000 or CNY 80,000 creates different exposure. A 10% decline in the same stock produces approximate losses of CNY 1,000 and CNY 8,000 before fees.

One profitable trade does not prove that the decision process was sound. A loss does not automatically mean that the lesson was failed.

## Plan before the result

In **Reflection**, write what you intend to test, the allocation you chose, and what could go wrong. After the result, compare it with the plan and explain what you would improve.

## Completion

Save a tutorial plan and reflection of at least 10 characters each, then answer both independent questions. Learning rewards thoughtful practice rather than trading frequency or returns.
