# Read prices and market data

Your goal is to find the information on a price chart without confusing it with a prediction.

## Four prices and one quantity

A candlestick summarizes a period's **open, close, high, and low**. **Volume** is the number of shares traded in that period.

Switch between daily, weekly, and monthly views. Longer periods aggregate the same available observations; they do not reveal future prices.

## Calculate a price change

A move from CNY 20 to CNY 21 is:

**(21 − 20) ÷ 20 × 100% = 5%**

Price changes and account returns differ. The account's allocation, execution price, and fees also matter.

## What volume can tell you

Higher volume means more shares traded in that period. It does not, by itself, establish that the price will rise or that buying is risk-free.
